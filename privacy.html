Privacy Notice

Privacy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

RenoLeads presents published land-lot information and sends inquiries into the shared NJ125 operations system. This notice describes the information processed when you browse the site or submit an inquiry.

Published property information

Available listing information shown on RenoLeads is retrieved from the NJ125 Supabase backend. RenoLeads does not maintain a separate property database or substitute mock listings when the NJ125 service is unavailable.

Information you provide

If you submit an inquiry, the form may collect your name, mobile number, email address, property or area of interest, inquiry type, preferred contact method, preferred date, and message. The information is sent directly to NJ125 so an authorized staff member can respond to your request and coordinate a conversation or site visit.

Consent and inquiry records

Submitting the form requires your consent to this Privacy Notice. The system records the Privacy Notice version associated with the inquiry. If the server cannot accept the inquiry, RenoLeads does not save your name, mobile number, email address, or message in browser local storage as a fallback.

Attribution information

For an inquiry, the site may submit the landing page, referring page, and standard UTM campaign parameters when present. These values help NJ125 understand where the inquiry originated. First-touch attribution is kept in session storage for the current browser session and is sent only when you submit an inquiry.

Local device storage

Saved lot identifiers and recently viewed listing identifiers may be stored in your browser on the current device. These convenience records do not contain the inquiry form's name, phone number, email address, or message. They are not a substitute for an account and may be cleared when browser storage is cleared.

Service providers

RenoLeads uses the shared NJ125 Supabase backend for published property data and inquiry processing. In this Phase 2 integration, RenoLeads does not use Firebase Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Functions, or Firebase Analytics as an application backend.

Property information

Listing descriptions, photos, displayed pricing, and location information should be treated as information to review, not as a guarantee. Verify ownership, boundaries, access, documents, utilities, payment terms, and other material facts with appropriate professionals before making a decision.

Contact

For questions about this notice or a listing, use the contact form or a configured direct contact channel shown on the site.
